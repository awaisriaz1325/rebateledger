# Market and competitor research (2026-11-13)

Desk research only (web search). Most sources are vendor marketing or listicles; prices are
unverified estimates. Use for direction, not for sales claims.

## Competitors
| Segment | Examples | Fit | Cost / setup (unverified) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise rebate platforms | Enable (acquired Flintfox Jan 2025), Vistex, Vendavo, Pricefx, IMA360 | Large distributors; one roundup says Enable suits 100+ active supplier rebates | Not published; estimates from ~$10k to $200k+/yr; 6–18 month implementations for large suites |
| ERP modules | Epicor Prophet 21 (Price Agreement Management), NetSuite (extra Oracle bundle), 365VendorRebates for Dynamics 365, Infor | Customers already on that ERP | Tied to ERP; partner implementation |
| BI with rebate tracking | Phocas | Mid-market distributors | Broader than rebates |
| AI-first newcomers | Rebate.AI, Kognitos | Mid-market | Not evaluated (no AI in RebateLedger v1) |
| Spreadsheets | — | Most small/mid independents | Error-prone |

## Demand signal
- Enable's 2024 survey (vendor-commissioned): 52% of distributors believe they do not receive
  all rebates they earn. Perceived, not audited.
- No independent study quantifies revenue lost to unclaimed rebates.

## Gap for RebateLedger
Between spreadsheets and enterprise platforms: fast file-based setup, audit-grade
"earned vs received vs missing", priced for independents.

## Features commonly sold by competitors
Tier attainment and forecasting; deal modelling before signing; accrual to GL; claim
generation with proof; supplier/partner portals; SPA / ship-and-debit claims (electrical,
HVAC); growth rebates against baselines.
