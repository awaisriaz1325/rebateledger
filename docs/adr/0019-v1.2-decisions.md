# ADR 0019 — v1.2 founder decisions (recovery audit, buying-group fee)

Status: accepted (founder, 2026-11-17)

## D1 — Look-back recovery audit (Phase 20)
Free in pilots. No plan-catalogue or invoice change (SPEC §4.14 unchanged). The audit is
available to every organization; it reuses calculation runs (max three years) and reports.
Identified amounts are never presented as recovered (SPEC §1.4.5).

## D3 — Buying-group fee (Phase 23)
A buying group keeps one fee, expressed as a percentage of each rebate it passes through,
set per group. Per-supplier rates or fees on purchases are out of scope until a pilot shows a
real statement that needs them.
